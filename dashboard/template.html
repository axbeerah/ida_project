What-if · mart.what_if_base (last 12 months of run-rates)

What happens to stock if demand rises or the next supply is delayed?

+20%
60 days
FormalOutflow′ = AvgDailyShipped × (1 + Δdemand)
DaysOfCover′ = CurrentStock ÷ Outflow′
Shortfall = max(0, Outflow′ × Delay − CurrentStock)
Revenue at risk = Shortfall × NetPricePerPack
In plain wordsTake how many packs leave the warehouses per day, scale it up by the demand change, and see how many days today's stock lasts. If the delivery is late by more days than that, the packs you cannot ship are the shortfall, and multiplying by the average selling price estimates the sales lost.
Resampling · mart.kpi_confidence_intervals (bootstrap)

How precise are the yearly sales figures?

FormalPoisson bootstrap: in each of 200 replicates every transaction gets weight w ~ Poisson(1); the KPI is recomputed, and the 95% interval is the 2.5th–97.5th percentile of the replicates.
In plain wordsWe re-draw the 500,000 sales many times at random, with repeats allowed, and recompute each figure. If it barely moves, it is reliable. "± 1.5%" means a different but similar set of customers would very likely have given a figure within 1.5% of this one.
